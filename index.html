      How to use Song Ranker

      Compare two songs at a time to build your personal chart. Everything stays saved in this browser.

      1. 1. Add your songs

        On Songs, add tracks with a title and artist. You need at least two to start voting.

      2. 2. Vote on matchups

        On Vote, click the song you prefer. Use Skip or Too close to call when needed. Shortcuts: ← → to pick, Space to skip.

      3. 3. Check your leaderboard

        Leaderboard shows your ranked songs — or switch to Albums. Drag one song to any rank to adjust it manually.

      4. 4. Group songs into albums (optional)

        On Songs, choose Add album, drag tracks into the list, set the album details, and save.

      5. 5. Export or start over

        Export CSV downloads rankings. Reset votes clears results. Clear list removes all songs.

      Your progress is saved locally — close the tab anytime and pick up later.
